Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how Decision Compass works.

All tools are free and run entirely in your browser — no account needed.

Yes. Every tool on the site is completely free with no sign-up required.

Your answers add points to careers based on interest type, work style, strengths, and lifestyle goals. The top three matches show percentage scores based on total points.

The Tech Comparison tool compares two devices at a time. After viewing results, you can immediately select two new devices without refreshing the page.

You rate each option with stars and set priority weights for each criterion. JavaScript multiplies ratings by weights and shows the winner with a confidence level.

Tool inputs are processed locally in your browser. Only device favorites are saved in your browser's local storage if you use that feature on the Tech page.
